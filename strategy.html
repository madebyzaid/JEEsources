Preparation Strategy

  1. Finish the syllabus first, starting with NCERT.
  2. Make short notes for each chapter as you go.
  3. Practice questions daily, not just reading theory.
  4. Solve previous year papers under timed conditions.
  5. Review your mistakes after every test.
  6. Revise regularly, and sleep and rest properly.
